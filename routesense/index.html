THE PROBLEM

Knowing a transport route does not mean knowing whether a ride is actually available.

Informal public transportation can change depending on the time, location, demand, traffic, weather, and day of the week. Commuters may already know where vehicles usually pass, but they still face questions such as:

? “Can I actually get a ride here right now?”
? “Where is the better place to wait?”
? “How reliable is transportation at this hour?”
? “Will I still have mobile signal during the trip?”

Existing static route maps cannot fully answer these changing, real-world conditions.

THE SOLUTION

RouteSense is an Android-based mobility intelligence platform that uses sensors inside passengers' smartphones to understand how informal transportation behaves over time.

StopSense

Learns patterns related to passenger movement, pickup areas, and service availability.

SignalTrail

Measures cellular connectivity along frequently used transport corridors.

Instead of simply showing commuters where a route exists, RouteSense estimates:

Where rides are likely available
When they are available
How reliable the estimate is
How connected the journey may be
4

HOW IT WORKS

01
Opt In

The commuter voluntarily enables passive mobility sensing.

02
Sense Movement

The phone detects activity changes such as walking, waiting, and travelling in a vehicle.

03
Collect Context

GPS/GNSS, accelerometer, gyroscope, time, and cellular signal measurements provide mobility context.

04
Analyze On-Device

RouteSense estimates probable boarding areas, travel corridors, movement patterns, and connectivity conditions.

05
Protect Privacy

Detailed raw movement data is minimized and converted into anonymous or aggregated observations.

06
Build Community Intelligence

Observations from many trips reveal service patterns, pickup activity, reliability, and connectivity.

07
Recommend

RouteSense suggests transportation options based on current and historical conditions.

No dedicated GPS tracker or driver application is required.
Smartphone Sensor Fusion: Combines multiple phone sensors for movement understanding beyond GPS alone.
Activity Recognition: Identifies transitions: Walking → Waiting → In Vehicle → Walking.
GNSS / Geospatial Analytics: Analyzes movement trajectories and spatial patterns.
Cellular Signal Intelligence: Measures network conditions during real passenger journeys.
Context-Aware Computing: Considers location, time, activity, and environment when interpreting mobility.
On-Device Inference: Processes sensitive movement data locally before sharing minimized results.
Geospatial Clustering: Finds recurring pickup areas, transport corridors, and mobility hotspots.
Predictive Analytics: Estimates service probability based on historical and recent observations.
Privacy-Preserving Aggregation: Builds community mobility intelligence without publishing individual travel histories.
