THE PROBLEM

Knowing a route does not mean knowing if a ride is available right now.

Informal transit changes by time, traffic, and weather. Commuters face daily uncertainty:

? “Can I actually get a ride here right now?”
? “Where is the better place to wait?”
? “How reliable is transportation at this hour?”
? “Will I have mobile signal along the trip?”

EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES & SENSORS

Zero vehicle installation • No driver app or GPS tracker required
Sensor Fusion & Activity Recognition: Combines IMU + GNSS to detect Walking → Waiting → In Vehicle.
Cellular Signal Intelligence: Maps network QoS during transit journeys.
Geospatial Clustering & ML: PostGIS + H3 hexagons with XGBoost predictive analytics.
Privacy-Preserving On-Device AI: Local edge inference without raw trip logging.
