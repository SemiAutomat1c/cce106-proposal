THE PROBLEM

Knowing a transport route does not mean knowing whether a ride is actually available. Informal transit changes with time, demand, traffic, and weather.

❓ “Can I actually get a ride here right now?”
📍 “Where is the better place to wait?”
⏱️ “How reliable is transport at this hour?”
📶 “Will I still have mobile signal during the trip?”

Existing static route maps cannot answer these dynamic real-world conditions.

2

BENEFICIARIES

Secondary Beneficiaries:

THE SOLUTION

RouteSense uses sensors inside passengers' phones to learn how informal transit behaves over time through two intelligence layers:

StopSense: Learns pickup areas, movement patterns, & service availability.
SignalTrail: Measures cellular signal quality along transit corridors.
